---
name: backtesting-integrity
description: Use when creating, reviewing or evaluating historical trading experiments.
---
Check decision-time information availability, data revisions, pivot confirmation, split boundaries, execution delays, cost assumptions, ambiguous same-bar fills (conservative), repeated testing and final-holdout isolation. Require a reproducible manifest (data hashes, commit, config, strategy versions). Flag leakage or unsupported results as blockers.
